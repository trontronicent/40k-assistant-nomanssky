# No Man's Sky Settlements Guide (Overseer)

Everything about the planetary settlements a player can run as **Overseer** (German: Aufseher einer Siedlung): how to get one, the stats, buildings and upgrades, decisions, production, attacks, Autophage settlements and every update that changed them.

**How this was checked (2026-10-03):** official patch notes on nomanssky.com (Frontiers 3.6, Beacon 5.70 feature page, Beacon 5.73 and 5.74, Cosmos 7.04 and 7.05) and the complete Beacon 5.70 patch-note list (shacknews.com), cross-checked against independent guides (GamerGuides/Ludo Guide, TheGamer, Gamepur, Sportskeeda, Dot Esports) and Steam community threads from June and November 2025. Facts marked **(official)** are quoted from Hello Games' notes. Facts marked **(two sources)** were found in at least two independent sources. Facts marked **(single source)** or **(players, Steam 2025)** could not be confirmed a second time - treat them as a guide, not a rule. The community wiki (nomanssky.fandom.com) refused automated access, so it was not used.

**Important:** settlements were reworked in the **Beacon update (5.70, 4 June 2025)**. Exact construction costs from guides written in 2021-2023 are pre-Beacon and may differ in the current game.

## Getting A Settlement

### How do I get my first settlement?
Deutsch: Wie bekomme ich meine erste Siedlung?
After the opening tutorial missions, flying in space eventually brings a **distress call from a nearby settlement**; it places a beacon on a planet **(two sources: GamerGuides, TheGamer)**. TheGamer measured that this happens after "Awakening" and "Alone Amidst The Stars", about 2-3 hours into a new save **(single source)**. Land, talk to the settlers and fight off the Sentinels that attack; you are then sent to the monument in the middle of the town **(two sources)**. A Steam moderator thread calls this emergency-broadcast mission "The Settlers" **(players, Steam 2023)**.

### How do I become Overseer?
Deutsch: Wie werde ich Aufseher (Overseer) einer Siedlung?
"Submit your credentials to the monument at the heart of the settlement. If accepted by the citizens, you become Overseer of the settlement and may choose its name" **(official, Frontiers 3.6)**. In game the monument is the **Settlement Hub Interface** on the central pillar; choose the option to apply as overseer, then **invest in the settlement** to take over **(TheGamer)**. Before investing, the interface shows happiness, population, running costs and daily income **(TheGamer)**.

### How do I find more settlements? (Settlement Chart)
Deutsch: Wie finde ich weitere Siedlungen? Wo bekomme ich eine Siedlungskarte?
Buy a **Settlement Chart** from the **Cartographer** on any space station (to the left of the Station Terminus teleporter, option "Exchange specific charts", at the bottom of the list). It costs **5 Navigation Data**; every other chart costs 1 **(two sources: Sportskeeda/Dot Esports guide text and a Steam thread, 2023)**. Navigation Data comes from Ancient Data Structures on planets (Analysis Visor) and is sometimes sold at Galactic Trade Terminals. If the cartographer does not offer the chart, the settlement mission has probably not started yet **(players, Steam 2023)**. The official Frontiers notes confirm that settlements are found "through missions or maps from Space Station Cartographers" **(official)**.

### How many settlements can I own?
Deutsch: Wie viele Siedlungen kann ich besitzen?
Up to **four** since Beacon 5.70: "Added support for becoming Overseer of up to four settlements." Each one has its own clearly-named management mission **(official)**. Before Beacon a player could run only one. The **Settlements Register** page gives an overview of all of them **(official)**.

### Can I give up a settlement?
Deutsch: Kann ich eine Siedlung aufgeben?
Yes: "It is now possible to retire as a settlement's Overseer" (Beacon 5.70) **(official)**. Beacon 5.73 fixed retired settlements lingering in the Settlements Register **(official)**.

## The Overseer's Office

### What is the Overseer's Office and what does it cost?
Deutsch: Was kostet das Büro des Aufsehers (Overseer's Office)?
The first building you must construct; from its terminal you run the settlement **(official)**, and it contains a base teleport module **(GamerGuides)**. Cost: **210 Silicate Powder**, then **5 Metal Plating**, then **3 Microprocessors**, one per construction phase, about **90 seconds** between phases **(two sources: GamerGuides, Gamepur)**. Silicate Powder comes from digging terrain with the Terrain Manipulator. Since Beacon, teleporting to a settlement puts you **inside the Overseer's Office** **(official)**.

### Can I upgrade the Overseer's Office?
Deutsch: Kann ich das Aufseher-Büro aufwerten?
Yes, like every building since Beacon. Players report that the office upgrades fast - about **2 minutes from B to A** and **2 more minutes from A to S** - and recommend doing it first **(players, Steam June 2025)**.

## Settlement Stats

### What do the settlement stats mean?
Deutsch: Was bedeuten die Werte einer Siedlung (Produktivität, Zufriedenheit, Bevölkerung)?
Five key stats **(official, Frontiers 3.6)**:
- **Population** - grows over time while productivity covers the upkeep; residences add room **(GamerGuides)**.
- **Happiness** - affected by decisions, disputes, requests, new arrivals and policies; settlers' moods follow it **(official + GamerGuides)**.
- **Productivity** - produced by buildings; must be higher than the upkeep for the settlement to make a profit **(official)**.
- **Upkeep (maintenance cost)** - grows with the population **(GamerGuides)**.
- **Sentinel alert level** - rises over time towards an attack (see Attacks) **(official)**.
Since Beacon **every building contributes continuously** to productivity and happiness (and population), so the choice of buildings matters more **(official)**. Each settler has an **informational stats page** with their personality **(official, Beacon)**.

### What is the settlement class (C to S)?
Deutsch: Was bedeutet die Klasse einer Siedlung (C, B, A, S)?
Settlements are rated **C, B, A or S**; most start at C **(GamerGuides)**. Higher-class settlements produce more valuable goods **(official, Frontiers)**. Beacon "completely reworked and rebalanced" the class algorithm: existing settlements may have shifted slightly, and reaching **S-class** is now "more achievable and predictable" **(official)**. Upgrading buildings raises the settlement's class **(official)**.

### What are settlement features?
Deutsch: Was sind Siedlungs-Merkmale (Features)?
Stat bonuses are awarded as **features**; stronger features replace weaker ones, and at the limit weaker ones become plain stat bonuses **(official, Frontiers)**. Beacon raised the limit to **eighteen** features (from six) **(official)**. Returning players received the unique **"Pioneer Spirit"** feature at their existing settlements **(official, Beacon)**. Some buildings give features that boost the whole settlement **(official)**.

## Buildings And Upgrades

### How do new buildings get built?
Deutsch: Wie werden neue Gebäude in der Siedlung gebaut?
Settlers frequently propose a **construction opportunity** as a management decision; it shows how much productivity it adds and what share of the population supports it **(official + GamerGuides)**. Construction runs in **phases**, each needing materials you deliver at the building's construction terminal, with a waiting time between phases **(two sources: GamerGuides, Steam)**. No new construction is offered while something is still being built or upgraded **(players, Steam November 2025)**. Players counted **10-12 buildings** at the start and **27-29** at the end, i.e. about **17 construction opportunities** per settlement **(players, Steam November 2025, one careful tracker; another counted 19)**. Building layouts are procedurally generated from player base parts, so every settlement is unique **(official)**.

### Which building types exist and what do they do?
Deutsch: Welche Gebäudetypen gibt es in Siedlungen und was bringen sie?
Building categories: Factory, Farm, Marketplace, Residences (three sizes), Saloon, Starship Dock / Landing Pad, Tower, Utility Module and Warehouse **(GamerGuides/Ludo Guide)**. Since Beacon several have a special use **(official, Beacon 5.70)**:
- **Tower** - scans for points of interest; since 5.73 it can also **sweep for distress signal coordinates** (official, 5.73).
- **Landing pad** - helps locate **crashed ships**.
- **Marketplace** - hosts an **envoy of the system's most prominent guild**.
- **Factory** - generates a **Multi-Tool box**.
- **Farm** - contains **planters**.
- **Saloon** - the Overseer can **prepare its menu**; jukebox bars let you pick the music.
- **Fishing pond** - can now be built at every settlement.
- A **merchant who builds custom starships** can appear (official Beacon feature page).
Every building has a **persistent terminal** **(official)**.

### What did buildings cost to construct? (pre-Beacon guide numbers)

| Factory | 1 h 30 min | 210 Magnetised Ferrite > 110 Gold > 3 Hydraulic Wiring | Productivity |
| Farm | 1 h 30 min | 210 Chromatic Metal > 140 Magnetised Ferrite > 2 Heat Capacitor | Productivity |
| Marketplace | 2 h | 340 Silicate Powder > 2 Quantum Computer | Productivity |
| Residence (medium) | 46 min | 140 Pure Ferrite > 110 Chromatic Metal > 5 Metal Plating > 3 Glass | +3 population |

### How do I upgrade a settlement building?
Deutsch: Wie werte ich ein Siedlungsgebäude auf (C bis S)?
Since Beacon "individual settlement buildings can be upgraded, to enhance the appearance and class of the settlement" - higher class also brings more utility and a more prestigious name **(official)**. Start an upgrade at the **building's own terminal** or at the **administration terminal** in the Overseer's Office **(official)**; there is also a management decision that upgrades a building **(official)**. At the terminal choose "Supply <resource> to upgrade to B-class", then A, then S; each step consumes a resource and takes time **(players, Steam June 2025)**. Players report about a **2-hour wait** before the next building can be upgraded, and that a building now needs about six steps instead of three **(players, Steam June 2025)**. Upgrading buildings is also the main way out of debt: one player needed half the buildings at A-class and 4 real days to clear a debt **(players, Steam June 2025)**.

## Decisions And Settlers

### What decisions does an Overseer make?
Deutsch: Welche Entscheidungen trifft der Aufseher?
Every few hours the settlement asks for a decision: greeting strangers, resolving crimes and disputes, setting policies and research goals, new arrivals and construction **(official, Frontiers)**. Beacon widened every category and allows up to **four options** per decision (previously two); citizen disputes were completely reworked **(official)**. New decision types since Beacon: upgrade a building's class, commission artwork, name a newborn citizen, use the settlement's current **blessing**, receive a gift from a specialist worker, and - rarely - **surrender a settler for a bounty** **(official)**. Decisions can now reward **standing, alien vocabulary, procedurally generated products and nanites** **(official)**.

### Who are specialists and what are settler expeditions?
Deutsch: Was sind Spezialisten und Siedler-Expeditionen?
Specialist citizens can join (for example a **hologram architect** or an **astronomer**) and can be met wandering the settlement **(official, Beacon)**. Settlers may ask to go on **expeditions**; the request now names the expedition's leader, and the tracking mission shows what the party is attempting **(official, Beacon)**. Any settler can be inspected to see their current thoughts **(official, Frontiers)**. Citizens release **fireworks** after significant events, for example a successful defence **(official, Beacon and 5.74)**.

### What is the settlement history page?
Deutsch: Was zeigt die Siedlungs-Chronik (History)?
Beacon added a **history page** (Historical Records) with the settlement's accomplishments, events, happiness rating and population growth **(official)**.

## Production, Income And Debt

### How do I get resources from my settlement?
Deutsch: Wie bekomme ich Ressourcen aus meiner Siedlung? Was produziert sie?
When productivity is higher than upkeep, the settlement produces goods that the Overseer claims at the **terminal in the Overseer's Office** **(official, Frontiers)**. Since Beacon the Overseer **chooses what the settlement produces**; the choices depend on the settlement's race and buildings, and **higher-class buildings unlock more lucrative products**, which can be sold **(official)**. Thriving settlements also raise the **wealth of their solar system** **(official, Beacon)**. Cosmos 7.04 (21 September 2026) fixed a bug where raising productivity actually **lowered** the production rate **(official)**.

### How does settlement debt work?
Deutsch: Wie funktionieren Schulden einer Siedlung?
A settlement can fall into **debt**, for example after commissioning expensive technology. Debt is repaid **automatically from surplus production**, and while in debt the Overseer **cannot claim products** **(official, Frontiers)**. Since Beacon an overpayment on debt goes into the settlement's **reserves** **(official)**. Cosmos 7.04 fixed debt suddenly jumping to about **2 billion units** **(official)**. Way out: more productivity than upkeep (upgrade productive buildings, avoid upkeep-heavy choices) and time **(players, Steam 2025)**.

## Attacks And Defence

### When and how do Sentinels attack a settlement?
Deutsch: Wann greifen Sentinels die Siedlung an und wie verteidige ich sie?
The **Sentinel alert level** rises slowly over time and triggers an attack; the Overseer must defend the settlement **(official, Frontiers)**. Guides describe an attack at 100 % alert with about **six Sentinel drones**; longer fights cost more settlers, and defeated Sentinels drop combat supplies **(single source: GamerGuides, pre-Beacon)**. Beacon **significantly rebalanced** attack frequency: the alert rises more slowly while the game is not running, and with several settlements the alert is **shared between them**, so more settlements do not mean more attacks **(official)**. Attacks now bring more enemy types - repair drones, summoners, heavy and corrupted drones as well as standard ones **(official)** - and the **swarming brood** (insect swarm) can occasionally attack too **(official)**. Fighting an attack no longer raises your own Sentinel wanted level (fixed in 5.70) **(official)**.

### Can my squadron defend the settlement?
Deutsch: Kann meine Staffel (Wingmen) die Siedlung verteidigen?
Yes, since Beacon: members of your **squadron scramble to defend** - the patch notes say against **pirate raids**, the feature page says "when towns come under attack" - and squadron pilots can be met wandering your settlements **(official)**.

## Autophage Settlements

### What are Autophage settlements and how do I get one?
Deutsch: Was sind Autophagen-Siedlungen und wie bekomme ich eine?
Rare **robotic settlements of the Autophage**, with Autophage names, on all inhabited planets (Beacon 5.70) **(official)**. Players who have **completed "They Who Returned"** may get an **invitation at their own settlement** to visit one, and travellers who have discovered the Autophage can become their Overseer **(official)**. They **always start with a specialist feature that lowers Sentinel attention** **(official)**. The town-centre monument has an **Autophage Synthesis Terminal**; there are Autophage settlers (with their own titles, dialogue and thoughts) and visitors with their own gifts **(official)**. Their settlers are **cloaked** and are revealed by scanning (5.73 fixed having to scan more than once) **(official)**. About **one hundred Autophage-styled base parts** were added for your own bases **(official)**.

## Update History Of Settlements

### Which updates changed settlements?
Deutsch: Welche Updates haben die Siedlungen verändert?
- **Frontiers 3.6 (1 September 2021)** - settlements introduced: Overseer, Overseer's Office, five stats, decisions, construction, production, debt, Sentinel attacks, features (limit 6) **(official)**.
- **Beacon 5.70 (4 June 2025)** - up to four settlements, retire, building upgrades and per-building stats, production choice, class rework, 18 features, Autophage settlements, specialists, new decisions, history page and Settlements Register, squadron defence, swarming brood, rebalanced attacks **(official)**.
- **Beacon 5.72 (12 June 2025; some sites write 5.072)** - fixed missions and planetary charts failing to locate Autophage settlements **(single source: mp1st)**.
- **Beacon 5.73 (1 July 2025)** - towers can sweep for distress signals; fixes for Autophage visitors affecting standing, monument collision, cloaked settlers, settlement data, the Register after retiring, tower recharging, medium dwellings without a terminal **(official)**.
- **Beacon 5.74 (15 July 2025)** - fixes with several settlements in the same system (tutorial, "Trace of Metal" mission), fireworks after a defence, Sentinel quads idling, terminal labels, PS5 generation **(official)**.
- **Cosmos 7.04 (21 September 2026)** - productivity increases lowered production (fixed), buildings without construction terminals (fixed), debt jumping to ~2 billion units (fixed) **(official)**.
- **Cosmos 7.05 (late September 2026)** - settlement buildings failing to place, Beacon milestone not counting manual upgrades from a building terminal (fixed), and shorter waits for building upgrades for the **Beacon milestone** of Expedition 23 "Our Journey Continues" **(official)**.

## Tips

### How do I run a settlement well?
Deutsch: Tipps für eine gute Siedlung?
- Build the Overseer's Office first and upgrade it to S (only minutes) **(players, Steam 2025)**.
- Early on, prefer buildings and decisions that raise productivity until it clearly exceeds upkeep; then spend on happiness, since happiness opportunities come less often **(GamerGuides)**.
- Choose production for what you need; upgrade the matching buildings to unlock better products **(official)**.
- Defend attacks on foot rather than from your ship where possible; guides call ground combat safer **(single source: GamerGuides)**.
- Several settlements do not multiply attacks - the alert is shared **(official)**.
