# No Man's Sky Resources and Crafting Reference

Quick lookup tables for resources, fuels and common recipes. Source: No Man's Sky community wiki (pages Refiner, Warp Cell, Antimatter, Warp Hypercore, Launch Thruster, Starship Launch Fuel, Di-hydrogen Jelly, Life Support Gel, Ion Battery, Glass, Terrain Manipulator, Activated Indium, Chromatic Metal, Starship Outfitting Terminal, Inventory), checked on 2026-10-02. Recipes can change with updates.

## Stellar Metals By Star Colour

### Which metal is found at which star colour?
| Star colour | Stellar metal | Activated variant | Hyperdrive needed to reach it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yellow | Copper | Activated Copper | none (base hyperdrive) |
| Red | Cadmium | Activated Cadmium | Cadmium Drive |
| Green | Emeril | Activated Emeril | Emeril Drive |
| Blue | Indium | Activated Indium | Indium Drive |
| Purple | Quartzite | Activated Quartzite | unlocked through the Worlds Part II mission "In Stellar Multitudes" |

Activated variants occur on extreme-weather planets and are worth much more. All stellar metals refine into **Chromatic Metal**; rarer metals give more.

## Fuels And Recharging

### Which resource recharges which system?
| System | Recharge with |
|---|---|
| Launch thrusters | Uranium (2.5% per unit), Starship Launch Fuel (100%); free on landing pads |
| Pulse engine | Tritium |
| Starship hyperdrive | Warp Cell (4 = full), Warp Hypercore (1 = full) |
| Freighter hyperdrive | Warp Cells or one Warp Hypercore |
| Hazard protection | Sodium, Sodium Nitrate, Ion Battery |
| Life support | Oxygen, Life Support Gel |
| Terrain Manipulator | Ferrite Dust (0.5%), Pure Ferrite (1%), Magnetised Ferrite (5%), Silicate Powder (0.5%) |

## Common Crafting Recipes

### What is the recipe for a Warp Cell?
**Warp Cell** = 1 Antimatter + 1 Antimatter Housing. **Antimatter** = 25 Chromatic Metal + 20 Condensed Carbon. **Antimatter Housing** = 30 Oxygen + 50 Ferrite Dust. Warp Cells cannot be sold.

### What is the recipe for a Warp Hypercore?
**Warp Hypercore** = 1 Antimatter + 1 Storm Crystal. Its blueprint comes from Manufacturing Facilities, Operations Centres, or the Synthesis Laboratory on the Space Anomaly (250 nanites).

### What is the recipe for Starship Launch Fuel?
**Starship Launch Fuel** = 40 Di-hydrogen + 1 Metal Plating. It refills the launch thrusters to 100%.

### What is the recipe for Di-hydrogen Jelly?
Craft from **40 Di-hydrogen**, or refine **30 Di-hydrogen** into 1 Di-hydrogen Jelly (better ratio).

### What is the recipe for Life Support Gel?
**Life Support Gel** = 1 Di-hydrogen Jelly + 20 Carbon.

### What is the recipe for an Ion Battery?
**Ion Battery** = 5 Ferrite Dust + 10 Cobalt. Refiner alternative: 10 Ferrite Dust + 10 Silver + 10 Sodium.

### What is the recipe for Glass?
Craft from **40 Frost Crystals**. Blueprint from Manufacturing Facilities, Operations Centres or the Synthesis Laboratory (250 nanites).

### What is a Stasis Device made of?
Quantum Processor + Cryogenic Chamber + Iridesite, which together need large amounts of plant and gas resources (Gamma Root, Frost Crystal, Solanium, Cactus Flesh, Star Bulb, Faecium, Condensed Carbon, Nitrogen, Radon, Sulphurine, Ionised Cobalt, Paraffin). It is one of the most valuable craftable products.

## Basic Refiner Recipes

### Which simple refiner recipes should I know?
| Input | Output |
|---|---|
| 2 Carbon | 1 Condensed Carbon |
| 2 Sodium | 1 Sodium Nitrate |
| 2 Cobalt | 1 Ionised Cobalt |
| 2 Salt | 1 Chlorine |
| 30 Di-hydrogen | 1 Di-hydrogen Jelly |
| stellar metals (Copper, Cadmium, Emeril, Indium, Quartzite) | Chromatic Metal |

The refiner output slot holds 4,095 in Normal mode (250 resources or 5 products in Survival and Permadeath); refining stops when it is full or out of fuel.

## Upgrade Costs

### How much do class upgrades cost?
At the Starship Outfitting Terminal (and the multi-tool upgrade terminal), on the **Standard** Purchases setting: C to B 10,000 nanites, B to A 25,000, A to S 50,000 (C straight to S 85,000). Expensive setting: 25,000 / 62,500 / 125,000 (212,500). Discounted: 3,300 / 8,250 / 16,500 (28,050).

### How much do inventory slots cost?
| Inventory | Price range | Free alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Exosuit cargo | 5,000 to 200,000 units | Drop Pods, Nexus missions |
| Exosuit technology | 1,000 to 125,000 units | Drop Pods |
| Starship (cargo or tech) | about 3 million to 75 million units | Storage Augmentation |
| Freighter | not for units | Cargo Bulkhead at Upgrade Control |

### How much does one inventory slot hold?
Exosuit cargo: 9,999 units of a resource in Normal mode, 500 in Survival and Permadeath; products stack to 20-40 depending on the product; 10 technology upgrades per slot.

## Currencies

### What are the three currencies?
**Units** (main currency: trading, ships, slots), **Nanite Clusters** (technology blueprints, upgrade modules, class upgrades) and **Quicksilver** (cosmetics at the Quicksilver Synthesis Companion on the Space Anomaly, earned mainly from daily Nexus missions).
