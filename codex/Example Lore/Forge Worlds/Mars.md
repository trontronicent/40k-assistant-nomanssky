# Mars, the Red Planet

Mars is the first and greatest of the Forge Worlds and the seat of the
Fabricator-General, head of the Adeptus Mechanicus.

## Role

- Principal source of the Imperium's Standard Template Construct (STC)
  knowledge and of Titan Legions.
- Bound to Terra by the Treaty of Olympus, which made the Mechanicus an
  equal partner of the Imperium rather than its subject.

## Notable sites

- **Olympus Mons**: the Fabricator-General's seat.
- **The Noctis Labyrinth**: vaults whose contents are recorded nowhere.
