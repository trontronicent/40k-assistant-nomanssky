# No Man's Sky Update History 2025 - 2026

COSMOS (7.0) and its patches have their own document in this library.

## Worlds Part II (5.50)

### What did Worlds Part II add?
Released **29 January 2025** (Switch: 30 January). It added a new class of **purple star systems**. Their planets include **gas giants** (planet-wide storms, warped gravity, moons in orbit), **water worlds** (kilometre-deep oceans that need special aquatic landing technology), rare colossal non-gas-giant worlds, and deep water on other planets and moons. All planets in purple systems have **Quartzite**, a new stellar metal, plus new terrain deposits, gases and refiner recipes. Terrain generation was also reworked for wilder landscapes on untouched planets.

## Relics (5.60)

### What did Relics add?
Released **26 March 2025** (PS4: 28 March). **Fossil collecting**: fossil beds contain specific bones that can be placed as base decorations or reassembled into complete skeletons on display plinths, then packaged, traded or sold. It added planetary **dig-site** buildings, a **fossil collector** on space stations who exchanges fossils, **stone guardians** that protect fossil beds (a huge living statue and a whirling rock storm), a rare skeletal lifeform and the **titanic bone-worm**. Expedition 18 "Relics" rewarded a living stone suit set, the Basilisk Crown staff, a skeletal companion and the Living Stone jetpack.

## Beacon (5.70)

### What did Beacon add?
Released **4 June 2025**, alongside the Nintendo Switch 2 version. Settlement overhaul: rare **Autophage settlements** (with an Autophage Synthesis Terminal for void motes and building your own Autophage staff), being **overseer of up to four settlements**, retiring as overseer, up to **18 special features** per settlement (was 6), choosing what a settlement produces and exporting it for sale, and a reworked settlement class system.

## Voyagers (6.00)

### What did Voyagers add?
Released **27 August 2025** (Switch and Switch 2: 16 September 2025). It introduced the **Corvette**, a large, fully custom starship with a walkable, furnished interior and room for several players. Corvettes are assembled at the **Corvette Workshop** on space stations from hundreds of modules (habitation modules in three styles: Titan, Thunderbird, Ambassador; hulls, wings, engines, reactors, shields, weapons, stairs). Modules come from buried salvage containers, Derelict Freighters, pirates, freighter cargo pods, crashed freighters, frigate expeditions and mission rewards. Some interior modules work, such as the Living Wall, Nutrition Unit, Refiner Unit and Mission Radar. Expedition 19 "Corvette" accompanied it.

## Breach (6.10)

### What did Breach add?
Released **22 October 2025** (PC, Mac, PS5). It continued the Corvette update: new corvette modules salvaged from **deep-space corvette wrecks** and planetary scrapheaps, plus space-walking and skydiving features. Expedition 20 "Breach" accompanied it.

## Remnant (6.20)

### What did the Remnant update add?
Released **11 February 2026**, the first update of 2026. Main features:
- The **Gravitino Coil**, a gravity gun for the multi-tool, available at the **Space Anomaly** or at planetary **Waste Processing Plants**. It grabs and launches movable objects: industrial waste, golden balls at ruins, spheres from the Sphere Creator base part, Sentinel combat supplies and even Sentinel drones (launched Sentinels are stunned briefly).
- **Industrial waste and salvage**: scrap worlds (planets with salvageable scrap) get waste piles and heaps and a new building, the **Waste Processing Plant**, where you deliver waste for recycled resources and rewards. Double-tap the Colossus horn on the plant's valuation scale to open its control panel. The Exocraft Radar on the Colossus finds plants and waste heaps.
- **Colossus customisation**: cabins with their own cockpits, a tippable flatbed, a mountable furnace (refine on the road), and 6 saved appearance presets at the Geobay (and at the player Appearance Modifier).
- New exocraft technologies: the **Cyclops Scope** (use the Analysis Visor while driving) and the Colossus-only **Excavation Blade** (harvests resources from obstacles you drive through).
- Corvettes became buildable on Nintendo Switch, and other players can help decorate your corvette's interior.

### What was the Remnant expedition?
**Expedition 21, "Remnant"**, ran for about six weeks after the update: convoys of players cleaning up a planet covered in wrecks. Rewards: posters, decals, titles, an animated gesture, three exclusive Colossus modules (camouflage finish, asymmetrical cabin, mechanical legs) and the five-piece Heirloom customisation set.

### Where do I get the Gravitino Coil gravity gun?
At the **Space Anomaly** or at a planetary **Waste Processing Plant** on a scrap world (Remnant update 6.20).

## Xeno Arena (6.30)

### What did the Xeno Arena update add?
Released **8 April 2026**. **Turn-based creature battles**: build a team from your adopted companions and fight creatures owned by aliens or other players. Each creature has species- and climate-based moves (attacks, heals, stuns, shields, status effects), can dodge, crit and gain experience. Its genetics (agility, health, battle traits) can be upgraded with **retroviral pellets**. Holo-Arena tables are on the **Space Anomaly** (multiplayer), planetary outposts, archive buildings, space stations (tougher "system champions" with better rewards) and some settlements. A new Traveller, **Iteration: Oceanus**, on the Space Anomaly teaches creature battles and offers a daily fixed-seed challenge. It also added the **Arena League** faction with ranked medals, titles and unique companions, and a **Creature Survey** mode for the Analysis Visor that shows creatures' battle traits.

### Where can I play creature battles?
At **Holo-Arena** tables: on the Space Anomaly against other players, and at planetary outposts, archive buildings, space stations and some settlement buildings against aliens (Xeno Arena update 6.30).

## The Swarm (6.40)

### What did The Swarm update add?
Released **27 May 2026** (PC, PS, Mac; Switch and Switch 2: 9 June 2026). It brought **Expedition 22, "The Swarm"** (about eight weeks): players were split into three teams that together built the **Prismatic Core** in the Space Anomaly through Purge, Restoration and Sabotage missions to unlock a final battle. New enemies: small **swarmer ships** and the huge **Hive of Glass** boss. In all modes, crashed swarmer ships can now be found on **dissonant planets**, guarded by planetary swarmers, and buried swarmer debris lies on worlds that are both dissonant and scrap worlds. Rewards included the **Direwasp** rifle multi-tool, Direwasp flightpack and a six-piece Direwasp set. It also improved third-person space combat (aiming, auto-follow, weak points that always crit).
