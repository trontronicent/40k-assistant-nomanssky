# No Man's Sky COSMOS Update (7.0) and Patches 7.04 / 7.05

Sources: official Hello Games patch notes (nomanssky.com/cosmos-update/, nomanssky.com/2026/09/cosmos-7-04/) and the 7.05 patch notes. Facts checked on 2026-10-02.

## Release Timeline And Latest Patch

- **COSMOS (version 7.0)** was released by Hello Games on **9 September 2026**, for the game's 10th anniversary. It is a free update that overhauls space: the star system map, deep space, space stations, orbital bases and spacewalking.
- **Patch 7.04** was released on **21 September 2026** (Steam first). It was a bug-fix patch: Atlas Path mission loop, settlement productivity and debt bugs, corvettes stuck in legacy space stations, memory crashes, Xbox One and Switch 2 memory optimisation.
- **Patch 7.05** was released on **30 September 2026** and is the **latest No Man's Sky patch as of 2 October 2026**. Additions in 7.05 (not just fixes):
  - **space station base resetting** (a Space Station Director can reset their station base),
  - **expanded recolouring options for space station exterior decorations**,
  - **external control buttons on Corvette hatches** (open/close a hatch from outside).
  - 7.05 also brought a significant PlayStation 5 memory optimisation and fixes for Beacon milestones, settlement construction, underwater ruins, derelict freighter completion, turret targeting, gravitino coil holstering, PSVR1/PSVR2 rendering and multiplayer networking exploits.

## Star System Map And Deep Space

- COSMOS adds an interactive **local star system map**, opened from the **Quick Menu**. It shows planets, the space station, deep-space points of interest and unidentified signals.
- Deep-space points of interest: rocky and icy **asteroid belts**, inhabited and infested **deep-space outposts**, debris fields, asteroid platforms and revisitable procedurally generated **hulks**. They become visible after the player has reached the Space Anomaly.
- The new tutorial mission that introduces the star system map and deep-space navigation is called **"Signals"**.
- Deep-space outposts are run by an alien **quartermaster** who trades maps and salvaged materials, and have a salvage and delivery terminal for contracts. Some outposts give rare station decoration blueprints.

## Shipbreaking, Tractor Beam And New Resources

- COSMOS adds **shipbreaking**: large space hulks can be broken up and salvaged.
- The new Corvette utility for it is the **Tractor Beam**, which gathers physical cargo into the Corvette hold.
- Three new raw materials harvested in deep space: **comet dust**, **gelatinous fibres** and **contaminated metal**. They can be processed and sold on the Galactic Trade Network.

## Space Station Directors

- A player can become **Space Station Director** of a station after proving themselves to the local authorities.
- A Director has the right to shape the station's **external decorations, interior layout and ancillary compartments**, and can expand the station by adding extra rooms to the main hangar. (Patch 7.05 later added station base resetting and more exterior recolouring.)

## Galactic Alliances

- **Galactic Alliances** are player groups for coordinated exploration and competing for territory.
- A player may join **up to three alliances** at the same time.
- Alliances are graded by member activity, and the most active ones appear on **leaderboards displayed at the Space Station core**.

## Orbital Bases And Spacewalking

- Players can build **free-floating orbital bases** anywhere in space, on asteroid platforms or in empty space; new corridor base parts were added. Some larger asteroids can host bases.
- Spacewalking: an **Eject** button in the Quick Menu now works for all starships (not only Corvettes), with true six-degrees-of-freedom movement including roll. Spacewalking drains exosuit cold protection.

## Expedition Twenty-Three Our Journey Continues

- The 10th-anniversary expedition of the COSMOS era is **Expedition 23 (Twenty-Three), "Our Journey Continues"**. It is NOT Expedition 14.
- It **started on 16 September 2026**, one week after the COSMOS update (9 September 2026), so it ends around **28 October 2026** (source: nomanssky.com/2026/09/expedition-twenty-three-our-journey-continues/, checked 2026-10-02).
- It runs for **approximately six weeks** and includes developer commentary by Sean Murray in the Collected Knowledge.
- Rewards: framed commemorative art, decals and titles, a **golden edition of the original Rasamama 36 starship**, an astronaut bobblehead, the **Starbound v0.27 multi-tool**, a vintage **Interceptor** ship and a **diplodocus pet**.

## Graphics And Performance In COSMOS

- **NVIDIA DLSS 4.5** support, enabling **Frame Generation 5x and 6x**.
- **Intel Xe Super Sampling (XeSS) 3** support.
- Foveated rendering support on PC VR.
- Console load times improved by about 6-9% through faster file handling; mesh data reduced by about 13%; bindless textures on Xbox reduce CPU usage.
