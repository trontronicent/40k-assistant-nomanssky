# No Man's Sky FAQ

About 100 player questions with short answers. Sources: the No Man's Sky community wiki (nomanssky.fandom.com: Upgrade Control, Cargo Bulkhead, Salvaged Frigate Module, Freighter, Frigate, Fleet Command Room, Matter Beam, Inventory, Exosuit, Drop Pod, Starship Outfitting Terminal, Hyperdrive, Warp Cell, Launch Thruster, Corvette, Sentinel Interceptor, Void Egg, Portal, Glyph, Nanite Cluster, Quicksilver, Settlement, Teleporter, Extractors and others), checked on 2026-10-02. Prices, drop rates and slot limits can change with game updates; for update-specific facts (COSMOS 7.0 and its patches) see the COSMOS document in this library.

## Freighters

### Where is the freighter upgrade terminal?
Deutsch: Wo ist das Upgrade-Terminal des Frachters (Upgrade Control)?
It is the **Upgrade Control** terminal, on the **central console in your freighter's bridge** (control room). It was formerly called the Freighter Research Terminal. There you trade **Salvaged Frigate Modules** for freighter and frigate technology blueprints and freighter construction modules, install **Cargo Bulkheads** to add freighter inventory slots, and recolour the freighter (10 colour schemes, 5,000 nanites each).

### How do I get my first freighter for free?
Deutsch: Wie bekomme ich meinen ersten Frachter kostenlos?
After some progress in the story, warping into a new system can trigger a **space battle**: pirates attack a freighter. Destroy the pirate ships, then land in the freighter's hangar and talk to the captain on the bridge. If you do not own a freighter yet, the captain offers you the freighter **for free**. You can only own one freighter at a time.

### How do I buy a different freighter?
Deutsch: Wie kaufe ich einen anderen Frachter?
Land in another freighter's hangar, walk to the bridge and talk to the captain. You can buy it outright or trade in your current freighter, which counts as two thirds of its value. Large S-class freighters can cost well over 100 million units.

### How do I expand my freighter inventory?
Deutsch: Wie erweitere ich das Inventar meines Frachters?
Install **Cargo Bulkheads** at the **Upgrade Control** terminal on the bridge. Each one adds a slot, up to a limit that depends on the freighter's class, so an A- or S-class freighter can grow larger.

### Where do I get Cargo Bulkheads?
Deutsch: Wo bekomme ich Frachtschotten (Cargo Bulkheads)?
The final reward of every **Derelict Freighter** (always one), destroying NPC freighters (about 30%), storage containers in **Crashed Freighters** on planets, Bounty Master missions in outlaw stations, and free from a **Merchants Guild envoy** at Exalted standing.

### Where do I get Salvaged Frigate Modules?
Deutsch: Wo bekomme ich geborgene Fregattenmodule (Salvaged Frigate Modules)?
Industrial frigate expeditions (7-20% depending on difficulty), Explorers Guild envoys (Master standing) and Merchants Guild envoys (Exalted standing), storage units in Crashed Freighters, crates in Derelict Freighters, Nexus and Mission Board missions, and rarely freighter cargo pods. Shooting cargo pods outside outlaw stations is a quick, Sentinel-free way to farm them.

### What do I spend Salvaged Frigate Modules on?
Deutsch: Wofür verwende ich geborgene Fregattenmodule?
At the **Upgrade Control** terminal: freighter technology blueprints (hyperdrive upgrades such as Amplified, Chromatic and Plasmatic warp shielding, the Matter Beam, the Interstellar Scanner) and freighter construction modules (rooms and staircases).

### How do I send items from a planet to my freighter?
Deutsch: Wie schicke ich Gegenstände vom Planeten zu meinem Frachter?
Install the **Matter Beam** freighter technology (5 Salvaged Frigate Modules at the Upgrade Control terminal). It transfers items between your exosuit and the freighter's main storage from anywhere, even from another galaxy. Freighter storage rooms are only reachable when you are in the same system.

### How do I bring my freighter to the system I am in?
Deutsch: Wie hole ich meinen Frachter in das System, in dem ich bin?
In space, open the **Quick Menu** and choose **Summon Freighter**. The freighter warps into your current system next to you.

### How do I build rooms on my freighter?
Deutsch: Wie baue ich Räume auf meinem Frachter?
Open the build menu while aboard the freighter. Basic rooms are available at the start; more freighter construction modules are researched at the Upgrade Control terminal with Salvaged Frigate Modules.

### How do I recolour my freighter?
Deutsch: Wie färbe ich meinen Frachter um?
At the Upgrade Control terminal on the bridge, for 5,000 nanites per colour scheme.

### How do I fuel the freighter's hyperdrive?
Deutsch: Womit betanke ich den Hyperantrieb des Frachters?
With **Warp Cells** or one **Warp Hypercore**, which fully charges a freighter hyperdrive. Hyperdrive upgrade modules, found in Derelict Freighters, add range and can reduce fuel use.

## Frigates And Expeditions

### How do I get frigates?
Deutsch: Wie bekomme ich Fregatten?
Once you own a freighter, fly close to a frigate in space (they usually escort NPC freighters) and hail it to talk to its captain and buy it. You can own up to **30 frigates**.

### How do I send frigates on expeditions?
Deutsch: Wie schicke ich Fregatten auf Expeditionen?
Build a **Fleet Command Room** on your freighter and use its terminal. You need one Fleet Command Room for each expedition you want to run at the same time (five rooms for five parallel expeditions).

### Why does my frigate need repairs?
Deutsch: Warum muss meine Fregatte repariert werden?
Expeditions can damage frigates. A damaged frigate has to be repaired before it can be sent out again. Fly out to it (or visit it from the freighter) and repair the damaged parts with the requested materials.

### What are Derelict Freighters and how do I find one?
Deutsch: Was sind verlassene Frachter (Derelict Freighters) und wie finde ich einen?
Abandoned freighters drifting in space that you board and explore on foot, room by room, for loot and logs. Force one to appear with an **Emergency Signal Scanner**, bought from the **Scrap Dealer** on a space station (the price increases with each purchase and resets daily), or an **Anomaly Detector**. The final reward always includes a Cargo Bulkhead.

## Exosuit

### How do I get more exosuit inventory slots?
Deutsch: Wie bekomme ich mehr Inventarplätze im Exoanzug?
At the **Exosuit Upgrade Station** on any space station or on the Space Anomaly (one slot per location per system), or for free by repairing **Drop Pods** on planets. Nexus missions also reward slots.

### Why can I only upgrade cargo slots at the exosuit station?
Deutsch: Warum kann ich an der Exoanzug-Station nur Frachtplätze erweitern?
The upgrade screen selects cargo by default. Switch the selection to the **technology** slot manually before confirming. Many players overlook this.

### How much do exosuit slots cost?
Deutsch: Wie viel kosten Plätze im Exoanzug?
Cargo slots start at 5,000 units and technology slots at 1,000 units; the price rises with each purchase up to about 200,000 (cargo) and 125,000 (technology) units.

### How do I find Drop Pods?
Deutsch: Wie finde ich Landekapseln (Drop Pods)?
Use an **Exosuit Upgrade Chart**: buy one from the **Cartographer** on a space station, get it from Ancient Data Structures, Minor Settlement vendors, trade outposts, the freighter's Galactic Trade Terminal, or as a reward for repairing crashed ships and machinery. Activating the chart in your inventory marks a Drop Pod. Repair it to choose one free slot upgrade.

### How do I recharge hazard protection?
Deutsch: Wie lade ich den Gefahrenschutz auf?
With **Sodium** (yellow plants), **Sodium Nitrate** or **Ion Batteries**. Sheltering in a cave, building or your ship also stops the drain.

### How do I recharge life support?
Deutsch: Wie lade ich die Lebenserhaltung auf?
With **Oxygen** (red plants), **Life Support Gel**, or other oxygen-based products. Ion Batteries and Life Support Gel can always be bought from Galactic Trade Terminals.

### How do I harvest hazardous or farmable plants?
Deutsch: Wie ernte ich gefährliche Pflanzen?
Install the **Haz-Mat Gauntlet** exosuit technology. Its blueprint is sold by the exosuit technology merchant on the Space Anomaly.

### What are supercharged slots?
Deutsch: Was sind supergeladene Plätze (Supercharged Slots)?
Highlighted technology slots in the exosuit, starship and multi-tool. Upgrade modules placed in them get a strong bonus. Put your most important upgrades there, and group upgrades of the same type next to each other for an adjacency bonus.

### How do I change my character's appearance?
Deutsch: Wie ändere ich das Aussehen meiner Figur?
Use the **Appearance Modifier** on a space station or the Space Anomaly.

## Starships

### How do I upgrade my starship's class?
Deutsch: Wie verbessere ich die Klasse meines Raumschiffs?
At the **Starship Outfitting Terminal** on an inhabited space station, next to the Appearance Modifier. It costs nanites; on standard settings 10,000 (C to B), 25,000 (B to A) and 50,000 (A to S).

### How do I add starship inventory slots?
Deutsch: Wie bekomme ich mehr Inventarplätze im Raumschiff?
At the Starship Outfitting Terminal, with units (from about 3 million per slot, up to 75 million) or with **Storage Augmentations**, one free slot each. The maximum depends on the ship's class and type. Use units for the cheap early slots and Storage Augmentations for the expensive later ones.

### Where do I get Storage Augmentations?
Deutsch: Wo bekomme ich Lagererweiterungen (Storage Augmentation)?
By scrapping starships at the Starship Outfitting Terminal (higher-class ships and haulers give them more often), from frigate expeditions, Crashed and Derelict Freighters, observatories, Harmonic Camps, space station mission agents, and free from Explorers Guild (Master) or Merchants Guild (Exalted) envoys.

### How do I scrap a ship I do not want?
Deutsch: Wie verschrotte ich ein Schiff, das ich nicht will?
At the Starship Outfitting Terminal choose **Scrap Ship**. You get units, any installed upgrade modules and sometimes Storage Augmentations.

### How do I get an S-class starship?
Deutsch: Wie bekomme ich ein S-Klasse-Raumschiff?
Watch ships landing at space stations and trade outposts and talk to their pilots; a pilot will sell the ship. Systems with a wealthy economy offer S-class ships more often. Alternatively upgrade any ship to S class at the Starship Outfitting Terminal.

### Where do I find Exotic and Solar ships?
Deutsch: Wo finde ich exotische Schiffe und Solarschiffe?
Both are rare. Solar (solar sail) ships are much more common in **outlaw systems** and at their pirate stations. Exotic ships appear at space stations in some systems. Look for documented ones in the community starship catalogue.

### How do I get a Living Ship?
Deutsch: Wie bekomme ich ein lebendes Schiff (Living Ship)?
Buy a **Void Egg** from the **Quicksilver Synthesis Companion** on the Space Anomaly for **3,200 quicksilver** and follow the "Starbirth" mission it starts.

### How do I get a Sentinel Interceptor?
Deutsch: Wie bekomme ich einen Sentinel-Abfangjäger (Interceptor)?
Find a crashed interceptor on a **corrupted (dissonant) planet**, purple-hazed worlds with Corrupted Sentinels. **Carrier AI Fragments** lead directly to a crash site. Unlocking the ship requires an **Inverted Mirror** (from Dissonance Resonators) and **Radiant Shards** (mined from the large purple crystals).

### How do I build a Corvette?
Deutsch: Wie baue ich eine Korvette?
On a populated space station, walk through the warp points on the left to the corvette platforms and use the **Corvette Workshop** console. You need at least landing gear, a habitation module, an access module, a cockpit, a reactor, a main engine and a weapon system. Get modules by buying basic ones at the workshop, trading spare modules, exploring salvaged scrap, or as loot from pirate battles, frigate expeditions, missions and freighters.

### What fuels my launch thrusters?
Deutsch: Womit betanke ich die Startdüsen (Launch Thruster)?
**Uranium** (2.5% per unit) or **Starship Launch Fuel** (100% per unit, made from Di-hydrogen). Landing on a landing pad or a marked landing spot costs no launch fuel.

### What fuels the pulse engine?
Deutsch: Womit betanke ich den Impulsantrieb?
**Tritium**, mined from asteroids in space. The pulse engine powers in-system pulse jumps.

### How do I fuel the hyperdrive for warping?
Deutsch: Womit betanke ich den Hyperantrieb für den Warp?
With **Warp Cells** (Antimatter + Antimatter Housing). Four Warp Cells fully charge a starship hyperdrive. A **Warp Hypercore** fully charges it on its own but needs Storm Crystals to craft. Warp Cells cannot be sold.

### How do I make Antimatter?
Deutsch: Wie stelle ich Antimaterie her?
The blueprint comes from the story mission after you build your first hyperdrive. Antimatter is crafted from Chromatic Metal and Condensed Carbon; Antimatter Housing from Oxygen and Ferrite Dust.

### How do I warp to red, green or blue stars?
Deutsch: Wie warpe ich zu roten, grünen oder blauen Sternen?
Install the matching hyperdrive upgrades: **Cadmium Drive** (red), **Emeril Drive** (green) and **Indium Drive** (blue). Each requires the previous one. Their blueprints are sold by the starship technology merchant on the Space Anomaly. Without them you can only reach yellow stars.

### How do I see a system's economy before warping?
Deutsch: Wie sehe ich die Wirtschaft eines Systems vor dem Warp?
Install the **Economy Scanner** (150 nanites at the Space Anomaly's starship technology vendor). It shows economy type and wealth on the Galactic Map and can locate trade outposts from the Quick Menu.

## Multi-Tools

### How do I upgrade my multi-tool's class?
Deutsch: Wie verbessere ich die Klasse meines Multiwerkzeugs?
At the multi-tool upgrade terminal on a space station, with nanites, the same way as starships.

### How do I get more multi-tool slots?
Deutsch: Wie bekomme ich mehr Plätze im Multiwerkzeug?
Buy a better multi-tool (from space station and trade outpost racks), use **Storage Augmentations** at the space station upgrade terminal, or choose the multi-tool slot reward at **Manufacturing Facilities**.

### How do I get a Sentinel multi-tool?
Deutsch: Wie bekomme ich ein Sentinel-Multiwerkzeug?
Sentinel multi-tools are found on corrupted (dissonant) planets, at Harmonic Camps and Sentinel-related points of interest. They usually need Radiant Shards or other Sentinel parts to repair.

### How do I recharge the Terrain Manipulator?
Deutsch: Wie lade ich den Geländemanipulator auf?
With Ferrite Dust (0.5% per unit), Pure Ferrite (1%), Magnetised Ferrite (5%) or Silicate Powder (0.5%). Switch its modes (Mine, Create, Restore, Flatten) with the secondary weapon button.

### How do I fish?
Deutsch: Wie angle ich (Fischen)?
Install an **Angling** multi-tool upgrade (added in the Aquarius update), then cast into water. Fish and Collected Flotsam are caught this way.

## Currencies And Resources

### How do I get nanites quickly?
Deutsch: Wie bekomme ich schnell Naniten?
Upload discoveries, discover all creatures on a planet, scrap or sell upgrade modules to technology merchants, loot Damaged Machinery and abandoned buildings, destroy Sentinels, complete missions, and loot Crashed Freighters.

### How do I get quicksilver?
Deutsch: Wie bekomme ich Quecksilber (Quicksilver)?
Daily Nexus missions on the Space Anomaly (the main source), Nada's story missions, and rare space encounters such as Condensed Stellar Ice. Quicksilver is spent at the Quicksilver Synthesis Companion on the Space Anomaly.

### How do I get Salvaged Data?
Deutsch: Wie bekomme ich geborgene Daten (Salvaged Data)?
Dig up **Buried Technology Modules** on planets with the Terrain Manipulator (2-4 each; not on dead planets or uncharted systems). Also from Minor Settlements, Crashed Freighters, companions digging, Merchants Guild envoys and missions.

### What do I use Salvaged Data for?
Deutsch: Wofür verwende ich geborgene Daten?
Base-building blueprints at the **Construction Research Unit** on the Space Anomaly (or a Blueprint Analyser in your base).

### Where do I find Activated Indium?
Deutsch: Wo finde ich aktiviertes Indium?
On planets orbiting **blue stars**, usually extreme-weather planets. It is a valuable resource and refines into Chromatic Metal. Other activated metals (Copper, Cadmium, Emeril) follow the same pattern on yellow, red and green stars.

### How do I make Chromatic Metal?
Deutsch: Wie stelle ich Chromatisches Metall her?
Refine stellar metals (Copper, Cadmium, Emeril, Indium, Quartzite) in a refiner. The rarer the metal, the more Chromatic Metal it yields.

### Where do I find Storm Crystals?
Deutsch: Wo finde ich Sturmkristalle?
On **extreme-weather planets during storms**; they glow brightly and show on the Analysis Visor. Not every extreme planet has them. Also from Mercenaries Guild envoys, Nexus missions and Derelict Freighter crates.

### How do I get Wiring Looms?
Deutsch: Wie bekomme ich Kabelbäume (Wiring Looms)?
Buy them at Galactic Trade Terminals or technology vendors at outposts, or dismantle obsolete technology and most upgrade modules.

### How do I make glass?
Deutsch: Wie stelle ich Glas her?
Craft it from **40 Frost Crystals** (from plants on frozen worlds); it can also be refined from Silicate Powder (dig it with the Terrain Manipulator). The blueprint comes from Manufacturing Facilities or the Synthesis Laboratory on the Space Anomaly (250 nanites).

### What is a good money-making method?
Deutsch: Wie verdiene ich gut Geld (Units)?
Common approaches: craft high-value products (for example Stasis Devices), set up mineral and gas extractors at a base, run frigate expeditions, trade between economy types, or farm freighter cargo pods. A Stasis Device is one of the most valuable craftables, made from Quantum Processors, Cryogenic Chambers and Iridesite.

### What does the refiner do?
Deutsch: Was macht die Raffinerie?
It combines resources into denser or new ones over time, for example 2 Carbon into 1 Condensed Carbon or 2 Sodium into 1 Sodium Nitrate. Medium and large refiners accept two or three inputs and allow more recipes.

## Space Stations And The Space Anomaly

### What is the Space Anomaly and how do I get there?
Deutsch: Was ist die Weltraumanomalie und wie komme ich dorthin?
A mobile station outside space and time and the main social hub (the Nexus). After the early story, summon it from the **Quick Menu in space**. It hosts the Nexus mission board, Quicksilver Synthesis Companion, Construction Research Unit, technology merchants and other Travellers.

### Where is the Nexus?
Deutsch: Wo ist der Nexus?
On the Space Anomaly. It is the multiplayer mission board, including daily quicksilver missions, played in groups of up to four.

### Where is the Guild Envoy on a space station?
Deutsch: Wo ist der Gildengesandte auf einer Raumstation?
On the **upper level, far right corner** of the station (also in Colossal Archives). Envoys give free rewards based on your standing with their guild (Merchants, Explorers or Mercenaries) and accept gifts to raise it.

### Where is the Mission Board?
Deutsch: Wo ist das Missionsbrett?
It is a stall inside every inhabited space station, offering secondary missions for the alien races and guilds, rewarding units, nanites, blueprints and reputation.

### Where do I buy base-building blueprints?
Deutsch: Wo kaufe ich Baupläne für den Basisbau?
At the **Construction Research Unit** on the Space Anomaly, with Salvaged Data.

### Where do I buy technology blueprints?
Deutsch: Wo kaufe ich Technologie-Baupläne?
From the technology merchants on the Space Anomaly (exosuit, starship, multi-tool, exocraft) with nanites. Upgrade modules are sold by merchants on every space station.

### What is the Scrap Dealer for?
Deutsch: Wofür ist der Schrotthändler?
A vendor on space stations who sells the Emergency Signal Scanner (which finds Derelict Freighters) and buys salvaged goods.

### What is an outlaw system?
Deutsch: Was ist ein Gesetzlosen-System (Outlaw-System)?
A system run by pirates, with an outlaw station instead of a normal one. Sentinels do not chase you there, smuggled goods and Solar ships are common, and a Bounty Master offers missions.

## Bases, Settlements And Teleporting

### How do I start a base?
Deutsch: Wie gründe ich eine Basis?
Build and place a **Base Computer**, then activate it. It claims a buildable area around itself, where you can build any base parts.

### How do I teleport between bases and stations?
Deutsch: Wie teleportiere ich zwischen Basen und Stationen?
Use a **Teleport Module** (Base Teleport Module, blueprint for 2 Salvaged Data at the Construction Research Unit) in your base, or the Space Station Terminus on any station or the Space Anomaly. You can travel to bases and stations you have visited.

### How do I set up automatic mining?
Deutsch: Wie richte ich automatischen Bergbau ein?
Survey a deep-level mineral deposit or concentrated gas cloud with the **Analysis Visor's Surveying mode**, then build a **Mineral Extractor** or **Gas Extractor** there, power it, and connect **Supply Depots** with supply pipes. All three blueprints cost 10 Salvaged Data each on the Space Anomaly.

### How do I summon my exocraft on another planet?
Deutsch: Wie rufe ich mein Exofahrzeug auf einem anderen Planeten?
Build an **Exocraft Summoning Station** (portable). Once a geobay for an exocraft exists in a system, you can summon it on any planet in that system from the Quick Menu.

### How do I get exocraft?
Deutsch: Wie bekomme ich Exofahrzeuge?
Unlock their **geobay** blueprints at the Construction Research Unit on the Space Anomaly (Salvaged Data) or through missions like "Exocraft Technician". Build the geobay at a base and summon the vehicle from it. The six are Roamer, Nomad, Colossus, Pilgrim, Nautilon (underwater) and Minotaur (mech).

### How do I become overseer of a settlement?
Deutsch: Wie werde ich Aufseher einer Siedlung?
Answer the settlement distress call that comes after the first tutorial missions, or buy a **Settlement Chart** (5 Navigation Data) from the space station cartographer. Defend the settlers, then apply at the monument in the town centre and invest to become overseer; build the Overseer's Office first. As overseer you decide on construction, policies and disputes, choose what the settlement produces and claim the surplus. You can run up to four settlements (since Beacon, 2025). Settlements periodically get attacked by Sentinels, and mismanaged ones go into debt. Full details: "No Man's Sky Settlements Guide" in the Guides category.

### How do I find a specific building on a planet?
Deutsch: Wie finde ich ein bestimmtes Gebäude auf einem Planeten?
Use a **Signal Booster** (portable) and choose "Locate Nearby Structures", or use **planetary charts** bought from the space station cartographer, which point to a specific type of site.

## Exploration And Points Of Interest

### How do I learn alien words?
Deutsch: Wie lerne ich Alien-Wörter?
Interact with **Knowledge Stones** (one word each), talk to aliens and answer them, use encyclopedia terminals in buildings and Space Station dictionaries, and follow ruins and monoliths. Each word belongs to the language of the system's race (Gek, Korvax or Vy'keen).

### How do portals work?
Deutsch: Wie funktionieren Portale?
Each planet has exactly one portal, even dead planets. Enter a 16-glyph **portal address** to travel within the galaxy. You learn to activate portals in the Artemis Path mission "A Leap in the Dark". Glyphs are collected from **Traveller Graves**. The in-game glyph list is under Catalogue & Guide → Collected Knowledge → The Atlas → Portal Glyphs.

### Where do I find portal addresses?
Deutsch: Wo finde ich Portaladressen?
In the community Portal Catalogue on the wiki or on portalrepository.com, which lists thousands of addresses by game version, platform, mode and galaxy.

### What are corrupted (dissonant) planets?
Deutsch: Was sind verderbte (dissonante) Planeten?
Planets of any biome with a purple haze, purple crystal clusters and **Corrupted Sentinels**. They contain Dissonance Resonators, crashed Sentinel Interceptors and Radiant Shards.

### What are Harmonic Camps?
Deutsch: Was sind Harmonische Lager (Harmonic Camps)?
Camps of the **Autophage**, Korvax beings disconnected from the Convergence. The mission "They Who Returned" introduces them and gives the Polyphonic Core for your multi-tool. **Echo Locators** (from Dissonance Resonators or Corrupted Sentinel Quadrupeds) lead to a camp.

### What are Ancient Bones?
Deutsch: Was sind uralte Knochen (Ancient Bones)?
Fossil sites (Natural Burial Sites) shown by an icon after scanning a planet. Excavate them to get valuable Excavated Bones.

### How do I find a crashed freighter?
Deutsch: Wie finde ich einen abgestürzten Frachter?
Use a crashed freighter chart from the cartographer, or follow distress signals. Its storage units contain Salvaged Data, Salvaged Frigate Modules, Storage Augmentations and Cargo Bulkheads.

### What is an abandoned system?
Deutsch: Was ist ein verlassenes System?
A system whose inhabitants have left: everything is still there, including an abandoned space station, but there are no NPCs and few or no ships. It is not the same as an uncharted system.

### Why are Sentinels attacking me?
Deutsch: Warum greifen mich die Sentinels an?
Sentinels police the planets and react to you damaging the environment, mining protected resources or attacking them. Escape their scan, hide, or fight. On aggressive Sentinel planets they react much faster. Corrupted Sentinels on dissonant planets are always hostile.

### How do I find a trade outpost?
Deutsch: Wie finde ich einen Handelsposten?
Use the **Economy Scanner** from the starship's Quick Menu, or look for the landing-pad structures on the planet surface. Trade outposts sell ships, multi-tools and goods.

### What are the three alien races?
Deutsch: Welche drei Alien-Völker gibt es?
The **Gek** (traders), **Korvax** (machine scientists) and **Vy'keen** (warriors). Each system belongs to one race, which decides the language and standing you gain there.

## Story And Missions

### What are the story paths?
Deutsch: Welche Story-Pfade gibt es?
The **Artemis Path** (main story: Artemis, the Fourth Race, the Atlas and the portals) and the **Atlas Path** (visit Atlas Interfaces and craft Atlas Seeds). The Atlas Path can only be completed once.

### What happens at the end of the Atlas Path?
Deutsch: Was passiert am Ende des Atlas-Pfads?
After the 11th Atlas Interface you hand over the last seed, the **Heart of the Sun**, and may choose to give birth to a new star.

### How do I get to the galaxy centre?
Deutsch: Wie komme ich ins Zentrum der Galaxie?
Warp repeatedly toward the centre on the Galactic Map, or follow the Atlas Path or Artemis Path, which offer a quick route to the centre at the end. Reaching the centre moves you to the next galaxy.

### What are Expeditions?
Deutsch: Was sind Expeditionen?
Time-limited community events with a fresh character, a fixed route of milestones and exclusive rewards (ships, multi-tools, decorations). Rewards are claimed at the Space Anomaly and become available on all your saves. The current one at the time of COSMOS is Expedition 23 (see the COSMOS document).

### What is the Nexus and how do I play multiplayer?
Deutsch: Was ist der Nexus und wie spiele ich Mehrspieler?
The Space Anomaly is the multiplayer hub. Join other Travellers there and start or join Nexus missions (up to four players). You can also join friends directly from the network menu.

## Pets And Companions

### How do I tame or adopt a pet?
Deutsch: Wie zähme oder adoptiere ich ein Haustier?
Feed a creature its favourite food until it is happy, then use the adopt option. You can also hatch eggs taken from creatures. Companions can dig up items (including Salvaged Data) and be ridden if large enough.

### How do I hatch a creature egg?
Deutsch: Wie brüte ich ein Kreaturen-Ei aus?
Collect an egg from a happy companion or a creature, then use an **Egg Sequencer** (the companion egg station on the Space Anomaly) to hatch it and modify its traits.

## Settings And Troubleshooting

### How do I change difficulty settings?
Deutsch: Wie ändere ich die Schwierigkeitseinstellungen?
In the Options menu, Difficulty Settings. Purchases (prices), survival elements, Sentinel aggression and more can be changed for most game modes. Prices for class upgrades (Expensive, Standard, Discounted, Free) depend on the Purchases setting.

### How many resources fit in one inventory slot?
Deutsch: Wie viele Ressourcen passen in einen Inventarplatz?
The stack size per slot depends on the mode: 9,999 resources per exosuit cargo slot in Normal mode, 500 in Survival and Permadeath.

### My mission marker is gone. How do I get it back?
Deutsch: Meine Missionsmarkierung ist weg. Wie bekomme ich sie zurück?
Open the Log (mission journal) and set the mission as the active mission again. A wrongly selected mission can stop Atlas Interfaces from reacting correctly.

### How do I take photos with glyphs or the HUD?
Deutsch: Wie mache ich Fotos mit Glyphen oder HUD?
Use photo mode for in-game pictures. Portal glyphs do not appear in in-game photos; take a system screenshot instead.

### What is the Galactic Map's filter for?
Deutsch: Wofür ist der Filter der Galaxiekarte?
It shows systems by economy, conflict level, race and star colour, highlights unvisited systems and your missions. Use it to plan warp routes (pathfinding to the galaxy centre, to a base or to the Space Anomaly).

### Can I play No Man's Sky offline?
Deutsch: Kann ich No Man's Sky offline spielen?
Yes. Multiplayer and Nexus missions need online play, and Expeditions need an internet connection.

## COSMOS Update (7.0) Quick Answers

### How do I open the new star system map?
Deutsch: Wie öffne ich die neue Sternsystemkarte?
From the **Quick Menu** (COSMOS 7.0). It shows planets, the space station, deep-space points of interest and unidentified signals. The tutorial mission for it is called "Signals".

### How do I break up a space hulk for salvage?
Deutsch: Wie zerlege ich ein Weltraumwrack (Hulk) zur Bergung?
Use the COSMOS **shipbreaking** feature with a Corvette fitted with the **Tractor Beam** utility, which gathers physical cargo into the Corvette hold.

### How do I become a Space Station Director?
Deutsch: Wie werde ich Direktor einer Raumstation?
Prove yourself to the local authorities of a station. As Director you can change its exterior decorations, interior layout and ancillary compartments, add rooms to the hangar, and (since patch 7.05) reset the station base.

### How many Galactic Alliances can I join?
Deutsch: Wie vielen galaktischen Allianzen kann ich beitreten?
Up to **three** at the same time. The most active alliances appear on leaderboards at the Space Station core.

### Can I build a base in space?
Deutsch: Kann ich eine Basis im Weltraum bauen?
Yes, since COSMOS: **orbital bases** can be built on asteroid platforms or in empty space.

### How do I spacewalk?
Deutsch: Wie mache ich einen Weltraumspaziergang?
Use the **Eject** button in the Quick Menu; since COSMOS it works for all starships, with full six-degrees-of-freedom movement. Spacewalking drains exosuit cold protection.
